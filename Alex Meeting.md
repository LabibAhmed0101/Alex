## PART 2 — WHAT THE CLIENT NEEDS TO DO

### WhatsApp Setup — Their Action Items

> Only relevant if their suppliers/customers use WhatsApp. If suppliers/customers use WeChat, skip this section and let me know plz.

Tell the client these are the steps they need to complete on their end. I will be handling everything after:

1. **Create a Facebook Business account** — takes about 20 minutes online
2. **Submit business verification documents to Facebook** — business license or IRS letter
3. **Wait for Facebook to approve** — usually 2 to 7 business days
4. **Provide a phone number for WhatsApp** — must be a number not already using WhatsApp
5. **Share their Facebook Business ID with us (that what we will be using to integrate with thier business)** — one number found in their account settings

> Once they complete these 5 steps and send the approval confirmation to us with the Facebook Business ID , we handle everything else.

> They should finish this before we start working on WhatsApp integration.

---

### WeChat Setup — Their Action Items

> Only relevant if their suppliers/customers use WeChat (more likely for mainland China suppliers/customers).

1. **Register a WeChat Work business account** — the business version of WeChat
2. **Provide business registration documents** to WeChat for verification
3. **Receive login credentials** and share with us
4. **Suppliers/Customers do not need to do anything** — they keep using their regular WeChat as normal

> In that case suppliers/customers stay on their regular WeChat. Only your side changes — your team uses WeChat Work, which connects directly into the system we building.

## PART 3 — QUICKBOOKS TIMELINE

Use this to set the right expectation on when QuickBooks will actually be live.

| What Happens | When | Who Does It |
|---|---|---|
| We create QuickBooks developer account | Immediately — no wait | US |
| Integration submitted to QuickBooks for approval | Around Month 2 | US |
| QuickBooks reviews the app | **4 to 6 weeks wait** | QuickBooks (Intuit) |
| Live — invoices and payments sync automatically | Around Month 3–4 | Automatic |

**Key message for the client:**
> "QuickBooks will not be connected from day one — it goes live around month 3 or 4 of the build. Until then, your QuickBooks account works exactly as it does today. Nothing changes on your end during that time. When we are ready, all you do is click one button to authorize the connection."

**Also the client have to know:**
> "The QuickBooks approval is reviewed by Intuit (the company that makes QuickBooks) — not by us. We submit it and wait. We cannot speed up their review. This is why we submit it early and let it run in the background while we keep building everything else."

### For the Client — Things They Need to Understand

**What they will have in 2 months:**
- A fully working invoicing and quoting system with branded PDFs
- AI that reads architectural plan sets and pre-fills the invoice automatically
- A sales pipeline where every deal, note, and client is tracked in one place
- Login accounts for every team member with the right access level
- Inventory management and barcode scanning
- Supplier order tracking and WhatsApp or WeChat integration

**What comes after 2 months (in order):**
- Month 3–4: QuickBooks sync goes live, Customer QR code pickup system with live tracking (actually we will be using quickbooks review time to do test thoroughly and finishing the left overs) 
- Month 5–6: Full financial dashboard, AI that learns their business and gets smarter over time



**Important limitations to set expectations on:**

- **WhatsApp and QuickBooks cannot go live immediately.** Both require external companies to approve the connection. These approvals take weeks and cannot be rushed. The client's operation is not affected while they wait — these are additions to the system, not requirements to start.

- **The AI gets smarter over time, not immediately.** On day one of the AI features, it works. But it becomes genuinely tailored to their business after months of real usage. It learns their pricing patterns, their clients, their suppliers. The longer they use the system, the more valuable the AI becomes.

- **WhatsApp only works if their suppliers actually use WhatsApp.** If suppliers are in mainland China and use WeChat, the WhatsApp integration does not apply. This is why we need the clear answer today.
